Your ESPN league, broken down.

Power rankings, playoff odds, graded trades, waiver report cards, live scores, rivalries and more for any ESPN fantasy football league. All of it is worked out right here in your browser.

Private league? Add your ESPN cookies
  1. Log in at fantasy.espn.com on a computer.
  2. Open developer tools (F12, or ⌥⌘I on a Mac) → Application → Cookies → espn.com.
  3. Copy the values of espn_s2 and SWID.

Or skip the cookies: your league manager can make the league viewable to the public in ESPN's league settings, and then anyone can open it here with just the ID.

Nothing is saved on a server. Public leagues load straight from ESPN. For a private league, your cookies pass through our relay to ESPN and are never stored or logged. They give access to your ESPN account, so only paste them on sites you trust. Read the code.

Trade ledger

Every trade, graded for both sides. Weekly bars show how many lineup points the trade added or cost that week. If a team flipped the players it got into another trade, the grade follows the chain.

How trades are graded. For each week after a trade, we compare the best starting lineup a team could have set from its real roster against the best lineup from the roster it would have had without the trade: players received taken out, players sent put back. Both sides use the same rule, so the grade measures the trade, not anyone's start/sit calls. If a team later traded away what it got, the follow-up trades are unwound too.
