Private league? Add your ESPN cookies
  1. Log in at fantasy.espn.com on a computer.
  2. Open developer tools (F12, or ⌥⌘I on a Mac) → Application → Cookies → espn.com.
  3. Copy the values of espn_s2 and SWID.

Nothing is saved on a server. Public leagues load straight from ESPN. For a private league, your cookies pass through our relay to ESPN and are never stored or logged. They give access to your ESPN account, so only paste them on sites you trust.
